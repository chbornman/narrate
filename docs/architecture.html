1What runs on the machine

One desktop process owns everything durable. Model inference lives either in supervised child processes (anything big, stateful, or crash-prone) or in-process via ONNX Runtime (only components that are tiny, deterministic, and stateless - a deliberate, narrow exception). Nothing here ever touches the network except model downloads, which are SHA-pinned and consent-gated.

the one durable process supervised child processes in-process models (ort exception)

2Where truth lives

Sidecars beside the images are the canonical data; everything else is a rebuildable index. The journal is append-only: nothing is ever silently edited, and redaction - the one sanctioned violation - is a physical scrub that propagates everywhere, byte-scan provable.

4The voice path, end to end

The one path with real-time constraints, and the most heavily verified: the binding rule snapshots your selection at the moment speech starts, so words follow your eyes even when you click away mid-sentence.

5Search: how a question finds a photograph

Keyword search works with zero models installed - the no-models mode is a complete product, not a fallback. With models, four signals fuse; your own words always outrank machine impressions (machine signals only ever ADD ways to find an image; they can be outweighed, never substituted for your words).

6Principles the boxes obey

Local-first, cloud-optional The free tier never touches the network. Downloads are consent-gated, SHA-pinned, license-gated before the first byte.
The journal is the product Append-only event log; entries never overwritten. Retraction hides, redaction physically scrubs - everywhere, provably.
Sidecars are the truth SQLite and PPVEC are rebuildable indexes. Export = the sidecar set. You can always walk away with everything.
Identity travels by hash Images are known by BLAKE3 of their bytes, never paths. Reorganize freely; annotations follow the pixels.
Collections over folders Folders are mechanical; collections are intent - evented membership with history, tags with time, never moved files.
Your words outrank the machine's Machine prose is retrieval fuel only. No visible AI captions, scores, or tags. Photoproof preserves YOUR opinions.
Degraded mode is a product No models, no GPU, no consent? Typed notes, pencil, ratings, FTS search - fully functional. Models only add.
Children die with the parent Supervised processes carry health probes, restart budgets, an orphan sweep, and a single-instance lock. No zombies.
